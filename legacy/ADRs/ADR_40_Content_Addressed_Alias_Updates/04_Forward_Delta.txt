WHY THIS PHASE EXISTS
`pfctl -T replace` re-ingests the WHOLE table and holds pf's rules write lock O(table size); a
single-IP change re-ingests millions of entries. Applying only the delta — `-T add` the additions,
`-T delete` the removals (the exact primitive Alerts already uses, pfblockerng_alerts.php:1499/
:1506) — makes lock-hold O(churn). "Force" then collapses to recompute+diff: no change ⇒ empty
delta ⇒ no-op.
