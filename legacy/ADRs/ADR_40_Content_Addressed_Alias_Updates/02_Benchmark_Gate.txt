WHY THIS PHASE EXISTS
The perf premise — "`-T add`/`-T delete` deltas materially beat `-T replace` at scale, and the
cost of recomputing every alias's desired set does not eat the saving" — is FALSIFIABLE and must
be measured against a baseline. pf is not real in CI (no pfctl in the unit suites), so this runs
on the ADR-04 live VM. The numbers decide whether Phase 4 is built, dropped, or the cross-list arm
is re-scoped (ADR §7).
