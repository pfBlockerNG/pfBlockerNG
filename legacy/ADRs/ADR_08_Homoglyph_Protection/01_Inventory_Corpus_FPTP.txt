WHY THIS PHASE EXISTS
The premise (mixing confusable scripts = malicious) is VERIFIED against ICANN v4.1 / EURid /
UTS#39 (ADR §1). What is NOT yet proven is that our detector has ~zero false positives on
legitimate IDNs — especially Latin+CJK (Japanese/Korean/Chinese), which UTS#39 deems
LEGITIMATE and a naive "any mix => block" would wrongly block. This phase measures FP/TP and
finalises the exact confusable-script rule, or records a pivot (ADR §7).
