WHY THIS PHASE EXISTS
`ip_blocklog`/`ip_permitlog`/`ip_matchlog` (and `unilog`'s IP-row passthrough) get their timestamp
from `pfb_daemon_filterlog()`'s `'BSD'`/`'syslog'` branch split. The `'syslog'` (RFC-5424) branch
already parses a full year-bearing ISO timestamp via `strtotime($f[1])` and then THROWS THE YEAR AWAY
reformatting it to `date('M j H:i:s', ...)`. The `'BSD'` branch copies pf's raw classic-syslog fields
verbatim — genuinely no year in the source. `dnsbl_parse_err` uses an ambiguous 2-digit-year
`m/j/y H:i:s` format. This phase unifies all of these to `Y-m-d H:i:s`.
