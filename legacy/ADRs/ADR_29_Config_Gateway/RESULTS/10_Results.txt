  covers all ~76 toggle-adapted fields over {'on', ''}.

## Reject-criterion outcomes

Per §7 reject criteria:

1. "A field's stored value cannot round-trip or downgrade losslessly and cannot be
   excluded without losing the gateway's value."
   VERDICT: NOT TRIGGERED. Every field round-trips cleanly. The one non-round-trippable
   field (pfb_idn / 'on'→'all' normalisation) is excluded by construction (null/null adapters
   at PfbConfig; normalisation only at pfb_global()). The gateway's value is not lost —
   the exclusion is the correct design per ADR-28 §2.2.

2. "Routing a subsystem through the gateway introduces behavioural regressions the tests
   cannot contain."
   VERDICT: NOT TRIGGERED. All 9 migration phases landed with full suite green. PHPUnit
   772 tests / 2780 assertions cover all routed paths. No regressions observed.

3. "The gateway measurably complicates rather than simplifies the call sites (net-negative
   on the maintainability premise)."
   VERDICT: NOT TRIGGERED. The gateway simplified every migrated call site:
   - pfb_global(): raw per-key reads collapsed into a single PfbConfig::read() per key
     (with registered defaults, no per-site defaulting).
   - pfb_remove_config_settings(): 22 config_del_path calls → PfbConfig::deleteSection
     per section.
   - All www/ $pconfig load/save paths: section round-trips use readSection/writeSection;
     individual field reads use PfbConfig::read().
   - The enforcement sniff now makes the rule mechanical, removing the convention-only risk.
