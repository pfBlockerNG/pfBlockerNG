WHY THIS PHASE EXISTS

dict: category (ipv4/ipv6/dnsbl) -> sub-category (PRI1...) -> feeds:[ {status, feed, website,
url, header, alternate?, past_urls?} ]. This phase distils it into a map, defensively.
