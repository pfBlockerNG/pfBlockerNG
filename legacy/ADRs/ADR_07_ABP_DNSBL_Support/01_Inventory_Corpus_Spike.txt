WHY THIS PHASE EXISTS
The premise (full ABP is worth doing) is ACCEPTED — the maintainer stated the pain
(dropped `@@` exceptions + regex => perceived over-blocking vs AdGuard Home). What is
NOT yet proven is that the implementation is safe/cheap enough: (a) how much feed regex
folds to dicts vs stays an irreducible per-query cost (ADR-01-class latency risk), and
(b) how the ReDoS guard must be built. This phase measures both and records a GO or a
documented pivot (ADR §7 reject criteria).
