2. PERF / ReDoS BENCHMARK ON adr/07 vs KILL-THRESHOLD  (GATE = GO)
================================================================================
Run: python benchmarks/spike_adr07_regex.py   (CPython 3.11, Linux, tracemalloc OFF)
On-branch numbers reproduce the Phase-1 spike exactly (no drift):

  metric                                  measured (adr/07)   threshold       result
  --------------------------------------- -----------------   -------------   ------
  regex reduction ratio (/re/ -> dict)    46% (18 of 39)      >= 30%          PASS
  per-irreducible added latency (neg q)   0.1647 us/pattern   "few us"/50 us  PASS
  budget-bounded irreducible count @50us  ~303 patterns       >> realistic    PASS
  ReDoS pathological caught/MISSED (cap)  1 caught / 0 missed catch catastr.  PASS
  worst CAP-PASSING first-hit (<=253 ch)  0.109 ms            <= 500 ms       PASS
  GIL demo: thread timeout cancels match? NO (216x deadline)  (confirms design) --

  Per-irreducible latency sweep (negative query, worst case, overhead-subtracted):
       10 ->  1.92 us | 50 -> 8.44 us | 100 -> 16.92 us | 200 -> 34.04 us
      500 -> 82.08 us | 1000 -> 164.71 us   => 0.1647 us/pattern (linear).
  Statically-flagged dangerous (nested-quantifier): 4 of 21 irreducible. The only
  pattern that ran >=2 s (([a-z]+)*\.example$) is nested-quantifier-flagged ->
  dropped by the opt-in cap; (.*a){20}$ ~43.7 ms also flagged. Every cap-PASSING
  pattern's worst first-hit <= 0.109 ms (three orders under the 100 ms evict ceiling).
  GIL demo: a join(timeout=1ms) waiter on a runaway (a+)+$ was forced to wait
  ~215.6 ms (216x) and the match ran to completion uncancelled -> a query-time
  thread/asyncio timeout is futile under the GIL; hence accept-one-slow-first-hit
  then EVICT (not vetting, not a query-time timeout).

DECISION: GO -- no reject-criterion (ADR §7) tripped. No pivot taken. Reduction +
the important_rules fast path keep typical deployments far under the ~303-pattern
budget; the static cap catches the catastrophic nested-quantifier shapes at load
with no execution and no corpus false-negatives.

Shipped runtime ceilings (defaults): warn 10 ms / evict 100 ms thread-CPU per match
(time.thread_time, jitter-robust); opt-in static cap drops length-/nested-quantifier-
over-cap patterns at load. Both apply to feed AND user pfb_regex_list. Eviction is
snapshot-iterate + evict-after-loop (thread-safe under the GIL).
