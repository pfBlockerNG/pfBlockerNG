WHY THIS PHASE EXISTS
Phase 1 landed pfb_archive_probe() + pfb_validate_archive() (unwired). This phase
calls pfb_validate_archive() at the TOP of each archive branch in pfb_download(),
BEFORE extraction. A corrupt/truncated/wrong-format archive that nonetheless
satisfied the file(1) MIME gate is now rejected early and clearly, instead of
reaching the decompressor and failing late and opaquely (ADR §1, §3).
