WHY THIS PHASE EXISTS
Today the matcher reads several independent module globals per query
(dataDB/zoneDB/whiteDB/regexDB + ADR-07's allowRegexDB/important_rules), assigned
one-by-one at init. A background swap that reassigns them individually lets a mid-
swap query read new-dataDB + old-whiteDB — a torn, inconsistent decision. Bundling
them into ONE frozen Snapshot whose single reference is swapped atomically (one
STORE under the GIL) makes the future swap consistent-by-construction, with no lock
in the per-query hot path. This MUST land before any background swap exists.
