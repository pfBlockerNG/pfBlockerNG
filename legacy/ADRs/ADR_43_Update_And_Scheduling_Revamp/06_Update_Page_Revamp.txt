WHY THIS PHASE EXISTS
The Update tab's Force/Update/Cron/Reload controls inherited the verb overload — they don't reveal
force-reparse-vs-reuse or which side. With the named API (Phase 3) + the ledger (Phase 2/4), the page
can present explicit scope+force controls, a per-feed/job "next run" view, and a clean run-now. This
is a FUNCTIONAL cleanup onto the new API, NOT a visual redesign.
