otherwise). This phase pins the §2.2 contract with an ADR-04 smoke and records the manual
checklist for what CI cannot cover (real calendar rollover).

ACTION PLAN
1. Add tests/smoke/test_log_rotate.py (ADR-04 harness). Scenarios (Given/When/Then, assert
   before AND after — no false pass). Use at least two logs to prove per-log independence:
   - GIVEN log_rotate_ip_blocklog=daily AND log_rotate_ip_permitlog=off, both logs seeded with
     lines, AND the marker entry for ip_blocklog set to a STALE period (yesterday) — capture
     the ip_blocklog inode (ls -i) first. WHEN the cron entry runs. THEN ip_blocklog is EMPTY,
     its inode UNCHANGED; ip_permitlog is UNTOUCHED; the marker entry for ip_blocklog now holds
     today's period.
   - GIVEN the ip_blocklog marker entry already holds today's period (same period). WHEN the
     cron runs again (logs re-seeded). THEN ip_blocklog is NOT emptied (idempotent no-op).
   - GIVEN a chrooted python log (e.g. dnslog) with a schedule + stale marker. WHEN the cron
     runs. THEN it is emptied, inode preserved, AND still owned by unbound.
   - GIVEN all log_rotate_<type>=off. WHEN the cron runs with stale markers. THEN no log is emptied.
2. If a Web-UI tier is in scope, assert the General page renders the per-log schedule controls
   (ui_render) and a POST persists the values.
3. Update user-facing help/doc as needed (public feature — no secret/PFBL detail).
