WHY THIS PHASE EXISTS
suppress() today explodes an exact /24 into 255 hosts and SILENTLY NO-OPS on
any other containing entry (proven, ADR §1.2). iprange --except is true set
subtraction with covering-CIDR output (8 entries for /24-/32, 24 for /8-/32),
and iprange is already shipped as ${pathaggregate}. Empirical iprange facts you
MUST design around (ADR §1.2): rc=0 on success INCLUDING a legitimately-empty
result; rc=1 on a missing/unloadable file; rc=0 even on malformed tokens, which
it treats as HOSTNAMES and DNS-RESOLVES (no flag disables resolution) — so both
inputs must be pre-filtered to strict IP/CIDR shape before iprange runs.
