WHY THIS PHASE EXISTS
Per-client behaviour needs DISTINCT client source IPs — the smoke harness probes on-box from
127.0.0.1 and has no built-in multi-source client simulation. This phase extends the harness
where feasible and writes the manual checklist for the rest, then closes the ADR's DoD.
