WHY THIS PHASE EXISTS
Phase 1 added the scanner (unit-pinned), Phase 2 wired it default-off. Live
pfb_download() runs shell/exec, so its gate is only provable on a real VM
(ADR-04). This phase proves flag-on rejects an HTML-error text feed and flag-off
is a no-op, and runs the OFFLINE false-positive survey that gates the ADR's flip
to Accepted (§7).
