WHY THIS PHASE EXISTS
The manifest deps (`php83` vs `php85`, `py311` or whatever the Plus Python becomes)
are the single guard against a wrong-variant package silently installing. They must
be injected at build time from a machine-readable source — not hardcoded — so that
when CE or Plus bumps their PHP/Python version, updating `ci-metadata` is the only
change needed. Phase 1's verdicts do not affect this phase; proceed regardless.
