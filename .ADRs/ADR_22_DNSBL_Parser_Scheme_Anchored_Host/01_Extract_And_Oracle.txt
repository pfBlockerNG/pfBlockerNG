WHY THIS PHASE EXISTS
The inline scheme-strip at `pfblockerng.inc:9675–9676` is two lines of code with no
function boundary. It can't be tested in PHPUnit without invoking the entire download
loop (which talks to the filesystem and network). Extracting it into
`pfb_dnsbl_strip_scheme()` creates a pure, testable seam. The oracle tests capture
the CURRENT behavior — including `weird://evil.com` → `'evil.com'` — as the before-state
for Phase 2's tightening. This phase is safe to land on `devel` independently: the
behavior of every feed line is byte-identical before and after.
