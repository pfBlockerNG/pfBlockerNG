WHY THIS PHASE EXISTS
Today four near-duplicate install_cron_job families (cron/dcc/bl/ss_refresh) each carry their own
cadence + (dcc/bl) inline rand(0,23) jitter. Replace them with ONE tick (≈*/15) that only reads the
ledger and dispatches due jobs — moving all scheduling logic out of crontab into our PHP. Cadence +
jitter MUST be preserved (feeds at their freq, dcc daily, bl daily/weekly, ss_refresh per-tick), the
ledger must survive a clean reboot (#468 set) and degrade to due-now-jittered when wiped (no boot
stampede), and offline catch-up must work (next_due in the past ⇒ due).
