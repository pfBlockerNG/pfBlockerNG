WHY THIS PHASE EXISTS
The ADR's whole premise is that Python can be the single per-line domain-rule authority reproducing
today's per-format output (ADR §1.3: parse() "reproduces today's per-format behaviour"). parse()
and parse_abp are NOT interchangeable (ADR §1.3) and there are KNOWN PHP↔Python divergences (ADR
§1.5: #752 undotted-254, #753 wire-caps, IDN). This phase builds the dispatcher (Option A: ABP-
shaped → parse_abp, else parse() plain/hosts) and proves it against the Phase-1 corpus. THIS is
where the premise is falsified or confirmed (ADR §7 reject criterion 1).
