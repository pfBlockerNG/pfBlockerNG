the EFFECTIVE block set (post-suppression/whitelist), must include DNSBLIP, and must
include Deny-action GeoIP continents (which already live in denydir, ADR §1.5) — while
Permit/Match/Native are exact unions of their own dirs. And "additive" must hold for the
whole power-set of the four toggles, not just the all-off and all-on ends.
